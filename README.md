# CivicPulse
AI-Powered predictive platform, designed for smart cities and usable by people from all ages.

Development scope
Civic-Pulse was developed while participating in Telefonica's contest: Líderes Digitales
The app obtained the prize for being one of the 4 best-developed apps in the context of the own event.

Brief Summary
Civic-Pulse was designed especially for paramedics and police officers use. It allows them to act efficiently and effective to reach
certain incidents as soon as possible simulating roads closure and traffic. Consequently, the correct use of the app could lead to professionals saving lives.
The application implements the basic functionality of an emergency app, with map and weather services connected to API's and REST-APIs,
as well as a login menu and a simulated Machine Learning mode to predict the best possible route to reach a destination from a specific
origin.
